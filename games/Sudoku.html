Sudoku

Easy 0:00

Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box holds each number 1 to 9 exactly once.

Play: tap a cell, then a number. Tap the same number again to clear it. White numbers are given and can't change. The clock starts with your first number and pauses while Rules or Settings is open.

Notes: turn on Notes to pencil small candidates into a cell. Placing a number clears it from notes in the same row, column and box.

Clashes (the same number twice in a row, column or box) glow magenta.

Hint (turn it on in Settings) fills the selected cell, or a random one, with the right number (green). Undo takes back moves one at a time. Best times count only puzzles solved without hints (an undone hint still counts).

Accessibility (Settings): a bar under the numbers you entered, if they look like the given ones to you.

Levels: every puzzle has exactly one solution. Easy and Normal need only simple steps; Hard needs smarter eliminations; Master has the fewest clues. Pick the difficulty in Settings; it locks once you start a puzzle.
