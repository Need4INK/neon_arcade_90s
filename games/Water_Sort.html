Water Sort

Level 1

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another tube to pour into it.

Pouring: the top colour moves only onto the same colour or into an empty tube, and only while there is room. Each tube holds four layers.

Goal: sort the liquid so every tube holds a single colour. Full one-colour tubes glow magenta.

Levels: every puzzle is checked to be solvable. Easy, Normal and Hard get more mixed; from level 13 Hard adds a ninth colour and leaves only one empty tube.

Undo takes back moves one at a time. Difficulty, Restart and Shuffle (a new puzzle at the same level) are in Settings, and so is Accessibility: a mark on every colour, if some colours look alike to you. Reset goes back to level 1 and keeps your best level.
