Atu
Discards · tap to take

You (green) vs CPU 1–3 (red, yellow, blue), rules from the Romanian Remy set. Tiles 1–13 in red, yellow, blue and white (the black of a real set), two of each, plus 2 joly ★.

Deal: 14 tiles each, the starter 15. One tile is turned up as the atu: whoever holds its twin (yellow dot) announces it and gets +50. If the atu is a 1 or a joly it's a double game: every score is doubled. Anyone dealt 3 doubles may reshuffle.

Turn: draw from the pile, or take the last discard and use it in a formation this turn. Then discard one tile. The starter's first discard is out of play.

Formations: a run is 3+ consecutive tiles of one colour (1-2-3 or 12-13-1, never 13-1-2). A set is 3–4 tiles of one number in different colours. A joly replaces any tile.

Opening (from your second turn): at least 45 points with at least one run, or a set of 1s on its own. Values: 2–9 = 5, 10–13 = 10, 1 in 1-2-3 = 5, 1 in 12-13-1 = 10, 1 in a set = 25, joly = the tile it replaces.

After opening (next turns): lay any formations and add tiles to anyone's formations; a joly only goes on your own. Once opened, with 4+ tiles you can take a discard from anywhere in the row (not the first) together with all tiles after it, if you use it this turn.

Jolly swap: place the tile a joly stands for and take the joly; you must use it this turn, and each joly swaps only once. For a joly in a set of 2, add both missing colours.

Few tiles: with 3 tiles you can only take the last discard. With 1–2 you must draw and can only add to formations. Players announce when they have 3 or fewer.

Closing: lay down everything and discard your last tile. If the pile runs out the round ends with no closing bonus.

Scoring: 2–9 = 5, 10–13 = 10, 1 = 25, joly = 50, closing +50, atu +50. Tiles you laid or added count +, tiles in hand −. Not opened when someone closes: −100. Closing with a 1 or joly doubles the closer's score (x4 in a double game).

Game: first to the target (500, 750 or 1000 in Settings) wins.

Controls: tap tiles to select, Lay down makes a formation, tap a formation to add the selected tiles or swap a joly. Undo takes back your turn.
