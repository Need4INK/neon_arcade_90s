Atu
Discards · tap to take

You (green) vs three CPUs (CPU 1 red, CPU 2 yellow, CPU 3 blue), rules from the Romanian Remy set. Tiles 1–13 in red, yellow, blue and white (the black of a real set), two of each, plus 2 joly ★.

Deal: 14 tiles each, the starter 15. One tile is turned up as the atu and shuffled back into the pile (the striped Atu box is only a reminder): whoever holds its twin (yellow dot) announces it and gets +50. If the atu is a 1 or a joly it's a double game: every score is doubled. Anyone dealt 3 doubles may reshuffle.

Turn: draw from the pile, or take the last discard and lay it in a new formation this turn (adding it to one doesn't count). Then discard one tile. Until you open you can only draw: you open with tiles from your hand and ones you draw. The starter's first discard is out of play.

Formations: a run is 3+ consecutive tiles of one colour (1-2-3 or 12-13-1, never 13-1-2). A set is 3–4 tiles of one number in different colours. A joly replaces any tile.

Opening (from your second turn): at least 45 points with at least one run and at least one set (house rule; the rulebook asks only for a run), or a set of 1s on its own. Values: 2–9 = 5, 10–13 = 10, 1 in 1-2-3 = 5, 1 in 12-13-1 = 10, 1 in a set = 25, joly = the tile it replaces.

After opening (next turns): lay any formations and add tiles to anyone's formations; a joly only goes on your own. Once opened, with 4+ tiles you can take a discard from anywhere in the row (not the first) together with all tiles after it, if you lay it in a new formation this turn.

Joly swap: place the tile a joly stands for and take the joly; you must use it this turn, and each joly swaps only once. For a joly in a set of 2, add both missing colours.

Few tiles: with 3 tiles you can only take the last discard. With 1–2 you must draw and can only add to formations. Players announce when they have 3 or fewer.

Closing: lay down everything and discard your last tile. If the pile runs out, the discards (all but the first and the last) are shuffled into a new pile and play goes on (house rule); with nothing to reshuffle the round ends with no closing bonus.

Scoring: 2–9 = 5, 10–13 = 10, 1 = 25, joly = 50, closing +50, atu +50. Tiles you laid or added count +, tiles in hand −. Not opened when someone closes: −100. Closing with a 1 or joly doubles the closer's score (x4 in a double game).

Game: first to the target (500, 750 or 1000 in Settings) wins.

Doubles swap: after the deal you may swap one tile of a double (two identical tiles) for one of a CPU's double of the same kind: small (2–9), big (10–13) or key (1). Select it and tap Swap, then Play. CPUs swap among themselves too. Can be turned off in Settings.

Board game (joc pe tabla): in your first 3 turns, before opening, tap Board (then Tap again). You keep everything in hand: no laying down, no adding, no joly swaps, and you take only the last discard (no need to use it). Close by discarding your 15th tile when the other 14 make a board hand:

Simplu: runs and sets, 500 · Bete: 2 sets of 4 + 2 sets of 3, 700 · Mozaic: 1 to 13 to 1, no two neighbours the same colour, the first 4 all different, the two 1s different, 1000 · Bicolor: two runs in two colours, 1200 · Duble: 7 doubles, 1300 · Monocolor: 1 to 13 to 1 in one colour, 1500. Jolys fill in anywhere. The board score replaces your tile count and closing bonus; atu and doubling still count. If someone else closes first: −100.

Player cards: ✓ opened · A holds the atu's twin (+50) · B playing a board game. CPU cards show tiles in hand (14▮) then the score; it turns magenta at 3 tiles or fewer.

Accessibility (Settings): a mark in the bottom-left corner of every tile, if some colours look alike to you: red one bar, yellow two, blue three, white none.

Controls: tap tiles to select, Lay down makes a formation, tap a formation to add the selected tiles or swap a joly. Undo takes back your turn.
