Othello

Place a disc so that it sandwiches a straight line (across, down or diagonal) of your opponent's discs between it and another of yours.

Flip: every sandwiched disc turns to your color. A move must flip at least one disc. Glowing dots mark where you can play.

Pass: if you have no legal move, your turn is skipped.

End: the game ends when neither player can move. The player with the most discs wins.

Undo: you can take back your last move until the other player moves.
