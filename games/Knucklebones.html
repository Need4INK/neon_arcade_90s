Knucklebones

Each player has a grid of three columns (four on the 4×3 board), three dice high. On your turn you roll a die and place it in one of your columns that isn’t full.

Scoring: dice in a column add up. Matching numbers in the same column multiply: two 4s count 8 each (16), three 4s count 12 each (36).

Attack: placing a die destroys every die with the same number in the opponent’s matching column.

End: the game ends when either grid is full. Higher total wins.
